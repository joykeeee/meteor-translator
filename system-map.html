Where each interaction actually goes

Five things you can do in the app, traced from your click to wherever the data actually travels. Two of them never leave your browser; the rest make a round trip through the Express server to Gemini and back.

  1. 1

    Load or switch an episode

    Pick a sample, or paste a video link with your own subtitle text. If you paste text, handleCustomVideo() sends it to Gemini to segment and translate; if you don't, a single placeholder line is used instead.

  2. 2

    Scan the video frame (OCR)

    “OCR Current Frame” or the Auto Scanner crops the bottom strip of the paused frame to a canvas, base64-encodes it, and posts it to /api/ocr-frame, where Gemini's vision reads the burned-in Chinese text and translates it. The result is inserted into the script, sorted by time.

  3. 3

    Analyze the whole script

    “AI Analysis” sends every Mandarin line in the episode to /api/analyze-subtitles in one call; Gemini returns pinyin, English, and Taiwanese-accent notes for all of them, which get merged back in while the original timestamps are kept.

  4. 4

    Ask the tutor

    Clicking “Ask AI” on a line or character — or typing directly — posts your message plus recent chat history and the line/character you're asking about to /api/chat. The reply is appended to chatHistory and shown in the drawer.

  5. 5

    Edit the script — stays local

    Searching, deleting a line, exporting to .srt/.json, looping a line, or jumping to a timestamp are all plain array operations (or a browser file download) on state already in memory. None of it touches the server or spends a Gemini request.
